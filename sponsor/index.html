Honky-Tonkin' Country Rockin' · Pittsburgh
Four guys from Povertyneck Hillbillies' famous run

Junior and The Push is raw, real country rock with the guitars cranked, built on originals. Frontman Junior Guthrie played lead guitar in Povertyneck Hillbillies through a national record deal, a Top 40 country album and a No. 2 video on Great American Country. Dave, Jeff and Ryan were part of that run too.

"This high-energy band drew 45,000 fans." The Vindicator, on Povertyneck Hillbillies
